Coverage and interpretation

WorldCover, UK geology and JRC water occurrence include regional selections. Soil maps describe only the upper 0–5 cm. Productivity and chlorophyll are 2020 snapshots. Counts measure catalogued observations, not true abundance or absence. Transparent pixels are nodata; zero observation counts use charcoal. Legends have fixed scales across resolutions; GeoTIFF values remain unclamped.

Official provider colours are identified individually. Other palettes are documented scientific or categorical choices, without claiming a universal official convention. GRDC maps retain noncommercial and contributing-provider licence restrictions.

Inputs that cannot directly produce reference maps

Weathering, erosion, soil development, spatial water chemistry and ecological suitability require additional process models and boundary conditions. They have not been fabricated from these references.
