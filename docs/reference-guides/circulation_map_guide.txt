CIRCULATION MAP GUIDE

Current palette contract: configs/climate-palettes.json (climate-colour-v2).
Positive fields: blue/cyan/green/yellow/orange/red. Signed fields: white/magenta/blue/cyan/green/yellow/red; zero cyan.
Black means masked/missing data. Sealed historical images retain their original palettes.
Standard reference renders: refs/processed/climate/maps/standard-v2/index.html.

Archive: runs/maps/climate/<quantity>/<season>/<layer>/{run_id}.png (see benchmark_map_guide.txt for exceptions).
Seasons: 1/2/3/4 = Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct; wind layers s/u = surface/upper.
The executable writes organized maps directly into runs/maps/. Internal diagnostics retain flat field identifiers. Reference previews and palette index: refs/processed/climate/maps/index.html.
ERA5 filename: era5_v{renderer_version}_{width}x{height}_{season}_{surface|upper}_wind_{lic|particles}.png
Seasons: January, April, July, and October representative snapshots.
Projection: global equirectangular; north is at the top and longitude is periodic.

surface_wind_lic
  Surface/boundary-layer wind streamlines rendered by line-integral convolution (LIC).
  Bright and dark filaments show streamline orientation, not speed or direction.
  Yellow arrows resolve LIC's direction ambiguity. The background uses the wind-speed palette below.

upper_wind_lic
  The same LIC rendering for the model upper layer, referenced to the 500 hPa circulation field.

ERA5 LIC maps
  Cached reference maps use ERA5 10 m surface winds and 500 hPa upper winds, bilinearly resampled to
  the simulation grid. They use the same LIC kernel, noise seeds, projection, arrows, and speed palette
  as the simulated maps, so visual differences come from the vector fields rather than rendering.

surface_wind_particles and upper_wind_particles
  White deterministic particle trails show forward paths through the surface or upper wind field.
  Trail density is a seeded visualization choice, not wind magnitude. Yellow arrows show direction.
  Background speed uses the shared positive palette: 0-25 m/s surface, 0-40 m/s upper.
  Land is brown-gray, ocean is navy, and coastlines are gray.

ERA5 particle maps
  These are possible because ERA5 supplies the same u/v inputs. They reuse the simulated maps' particle
  seeds and integration settings, and are cached by renderer version and output dimensions.

surface_wind_vector_error and upper_wind_vector_error
  Magnitude of the full vector difference |simulated - ERA5| in m/s, not separate component error.
  Shared positive palette spans 0-25 m/s. Yellow arrows point in the
  simulated-minus-ERA5 error-vector direction; subtract that vector to move the simulation toward ERA5.
  Coastlines are pale gray. Seasonal/layer summary metrics are stored with the run diagnostics.

surface_wind_speed
  Scalar surface wind magnitude in m/s. Shared positive palette spans 0-25 m/s.

surface_divergence
  Horizontal surface-wind divergence in day^-1, displayed over -1 to +1 day^-1.
  Shared signed palette: negative is convergence, positive is divergence; zero cyan.

moisture_flux_convergence
  Convergence of actual donor-plus-corrector moisture transport summed over both atmospheric layers in kg m^-2 day^-1 (numerically mm/day of water).
  Shared signed palette: negative is drying, positive is moisture supply; zero cyan.
  The display is clamped to -20 through +20; climate_process_fields.csv retains the unclamped values.

Raw fields
  New numeric process, weather and ocean fields are CSVs in runs/diagnostics/climate/{run_id}/maps/.
  climate_process_fields.csv contains transport and heating; weather_statistics.csv contains wind statistics;
  ocean_fields.csv contains ocean state. circulation_maps.csv records image export status, not field extrema.
  Vector-error metrics are in that same folder's wind_vector_comparison.csv.
  Historical raw rasters are organized under runs/fields/climate/<quantity>/<season>/<layer>/{run_id}.tif;
  historical numeric summaries remain in runs/diagnostics/<topic>/{run_id}.csv.
  LIC and particle maps support up to 2048 horizontal and 2097152 cells. Larger runs retain scalar and raw diagnostics but skip expensive flow rendering.
  PNG palettes are diagnostic, not categorical climate classes.

Grid resolution is selectable independently of these fixed diagnostic colour limits. ERA5 output dimensions follow the selected cell-centred comparison grid. Raw fields retain their physical values outside display limits.
